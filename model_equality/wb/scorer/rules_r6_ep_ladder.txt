Data. Copy of the ep- rows of the local ledger with http_status 200 (wb/in/ledger_ep_snapshot_*.jsonl),
44 to 46 responses per endpoint. Free-text rows: 21 per endpoint (LX01_story_long x5 and LX02_essay_long x5
at 256 tokens, tp_story x7 at 64, E02_pi_digits x2 and E03_story_zh x2 at 32). No random-stream probe.

1. Direction. loradir.py train on the responses of the first endpoint of the ladder only (the "sister"),
   tied to strength 1.5; recipe of R4 unchanged (rank 8, alpha = rank, all linear projections, dropout
   0.05, lr 5e-4, 12 epochs, cross-entropy at T = 0.7, rows ordered by sha1("train|" + id), first 20 % =
   validation, best validation epoch kept). No other endpoint is used for the fit.
   If the best validation gain is <= 0, the direction is declared UNUSABLE and the family INCONCLUSIVE.
2. Scoring. loradir.py score --profile-ctx with the context library of R4 amendment 4 (default, nosys,
   helpful, human, brief, expert, cautious, otherai, casual, nova), scales 0.3, 0.7, 1.5.
3. Statistic (R4c). Target s0.3, nuisances T and every context direction, free-text rows (and stream
   rows if any): z_mart, z_emp, s_hat = 0.3 x dose, standard error 0.3 / sqrt(Var).
4. Verdict per family.
   CONFIRMED iff (a) s_hat(second) > s_hat(third) > s_hat(witness); (b) z_mart >= 3 and z_emp >= 3 for the
   second endpoint; (c) s_hat(witness) < 0.13.
   REFUTED for an endpoint of the ladder iff the direction is usable and its s_hat is below 0.13 with a
   standard error <= 0.10: it does not carry the sister's adapter at a detectable strength (it may still
   be another variant).
   INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. Expected information is small (about 10 long responses per endpoint): the third
   endpoint (0.3) is expected at z between 1.5 and 3 only, so its significance is reported, not required.
5. Reported without decision: s_hat and z of the bare candidates (expected below 0.13 if they are the
   base; s_hat >= 0.2 with z >= 3 would name a possible carrier of the adapter); s_hat of the sister on its
   own validation rows; the selected context and T of every endpoint; the same statistic on all non-diag
   rows; the profile strength among 0, 0.3, 0.7, 1.5.
6. Limits stated in advance. One sister of 44 responses instead of two of 80: the direction is poorer than
   on the bench, so s_hat is biased towards 0 and a null result is weak evidence. The calibration
   "0.3 x dose = strength" was seen on one bench only. R4c itself still awaits its confirmation wave.
   Whatever comes out is reported as measured; any variant chosen afterwards is exploratory.
Order: qwen_B first, then qwen_A, then llama3 (its template carries a date: both date variants are added
to the context library, and the sister is fitted in the variant that maximises its base likelihood).
